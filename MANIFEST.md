## Governance

1. Internal source artifacts are not silently duplicated here as new canonical masters.
2. Exports should identify source identity, date, revision, and whether the item is SOURCE / OBSERVATION / INTERPRETATION.
3. Exploratory interpretation must not be promoted to fact without evidence.
4. No priority claim should be made merely from conceptual similarity.
5. Claims about LLM behavior must not be phrased as persistent learning unless directly supported.
